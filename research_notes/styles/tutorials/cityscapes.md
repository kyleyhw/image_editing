# City looks: tutorials with numeric settings

Research on 2026-09-26. Every number below was verified on the page. Many of the complete recipes
come from preset-seller blogs, which often give ranges.

## 1. Cinematic teal and orange (city or street, day)

- **lightroomtutorials.com, "Teal and Orange"** https://www.lightroomtutorials.com/teal-orange/
  - Colour Mixer hue: Red +100 (+50 keeps skin natural); Yellow -100; Green +100; Blue -100.
  - Directions only: the curve (fade the end points, then an S-curve).
- **Denny's Tips, "Orange and Teal"** https://www.dennystips.com/orange-teal/
  - Calibration: Blue primary hue -100; Red primary hue about +50.
- **Fstoppers, Jason Parnell-Brookes, cinematic orange and teal (Tokyo)**
  https://fstoppers.com/lightroom/create-cinematic-orange-and-teal-color-grade-lightroom-577820
  - Colour Grading: blending 21, balance 14.
  - Directions only: teal shadows, orange highlights.
- **Presetpedia, "Moody Japan"** https://presetpedia.com/japan-lightroom-presets/
  - Weak source.
  - Basic: Exposure +0.10; Contrast +30; Highlights -35; Shadows +15; Whites -10; Blacks -25;
    Tint +3; Vibrance +15; Saturation +5.
  - Hue: Orange +8; Yellow +20; Aqua -15; Blue +10; Green -20.
  - Saturation: Orange +30; Aqua +35; Blue +20; Green -25; Yellow -10.
  - Colour Grading: Shadows 195 / 25; Highlights 35 / 20.
  - Texture +15; Clarity +20; Dehaze +10; Vignette -20.

## 2. Kodachrome / Classic Chrome street (day)

- **The Editing Studio, Kodachrome guide**
  https://theeditingstudio.co/blog/kodachrome-film-lightroom-preset-guide
  - Basic: Exposure 0 to +0.3; Contrast +10 to +20; Highlights -15 to -25; Shadows +5 to +15;
    Whites -5 to -15; Blacks 0 to +10.
  - Vibrance +10 to +20; Saturation 0 to +5.
  - Hue: Red +5; Orange -5; Blue +5 to +10.
  - Saturation: Red +15 to +25; Orange +10 to +15; Yellow +5 to +10; Blue +20 to +30.
  - Luminance: Orange +10 to +15; Blue -10 to -15.
- **The Editing Studio, "How to Get the Kodachrome Look"**
  https://theeditingstudio.co/blog/how-to-get-the-kodachrome-look-in-lightroom
  - Contrast +10 to +20; Blacks 0 to -10 (conflicts with the guide above); Highlights -20 to -35.
  - Curve: shadow output 0 to 8; highlight output 235 to 245; pronounced S-curve.
- **Classic Chrome: The Editing Studio** https://theeditingstudio.co/blog/fujifilm-look-lightroom-preset
  - Contrast -10 to -20; Highlights -20 to -30; Shadows +10 to +20; Blacks +10 to +20;
    Vibrance -15 to -20; Saturation -10.
  - Saturation: Green -20 to -25; Blue -15 to -20; Red -5 to -10.
- **Classic Chrome: Cinemora** https://cinemorapresets.com/classic-chrome-lightroom-presets
  - Blue hue +15 to +20; Blue saturation -25 to -35; Green hue +5 to +10; Green saturation -10 to
    -15.

## 3. Matte / faded urban

- **The Editing Studio, Vintage Film guide**
  https://theeditingstudio.co/blog/vintage-clean-film-lightroom-preset-guide
  - Basic: Exposure +0.2 to +0.4; Contrast -20 to -30; Highlights -20 to -35; Shadows +20 to +35;
    Whites -15 to -25; Blacks +20 to +35.
  - Curve: black point up by 20-30 units.
  - Colour Grading: warm amber shadows, saturation 20-30.
  - Hue: Yellow +5 to +10; Green +10 to +15; Blue +5.
  - Saturation: Green -20 to -30; Blue -25 to -35; Aqua -15 to -20; Red -5 to 0.
  - Luminance: Orange +10 to +15; Yellow +5 to +10.
  - Vignette -10 to -20.
- **Fstoppers, "Create Faded Filmic Edits in 5 Minutes"** (portrait example)
  https://fstoppers.com/originals/create-faded-filmic-edits-5-minutes-lightroom-543247
  - Highlights -21; Blacks -29; Clarity +19; Vibrance +4.
- **Presetpedia, "Vintage Grit Urban"** https://presetpedia.com/urban-lightroom-presets/
  - Weak source.
  - Black point at about 20 output; Highlights -20; Contrast -15; Saturation -20; Orange
    saturation +10; Vignette -18.

## 4. Night city in the style of CineStill 800T

- **Lou & Marks, "CineStill 800T"** https://loumarkspresets.com/blogs/lightroom/cinestill-800t-free-download
  - Basic: Exposure +0.2 to +0.5; Contrast +15; Highlights -25; Shadows +10 to +20; Whites +10;
    Blacks -20; Tint +3 to +8.
  - Texture +5; Clarity -5; Dehaze +5; Vibrance -5; Saturation -5; Vignette -5 to -10.
  - Directions only: teal shadows and orange-red highlights at low saturation; less yellow and
    green saturation; halation.
- **Joe D'Agostino, "Recreating CineStill 800T for Lightroom"** (Philadelphia at night)
  https://www.josephdagostinophotography.com/joedagostino-photo-blog/2020/3/25/recreating-cinestill-800t-for-lightroom
  - White balance 2850 K; the other numbers are only in screenshots.
- Halation (Photoshop, a local effect that is not modelled): NUUGRAPHICS, FreeVisuals.

## 5. Tokyo / neon night street

- **DIYPhotography, Tim Daniels, "Wakui cinematic look"** (Tokyo)
  https://www.diyphotography.net/how-to-create-the-wakui-cinematic-look-in-lightroom-and-photoshop/
  - White balance at maximum (50000), tint 0.
  - Split toning: highlights 60 / 50; shadows 225 / 100.
  - Calibration: Shadows tint -100.
  - Directions only: curves (RGB toe up, Green toe up, Blue top down), lower contrast, higher
    clarity.
- **lightroomtutorials.com, "Masashi Wakui look"** https://www.lightroomtutorials.com/how-to-achieve-the-masashi-wakui-look/
  - Curves only, directions only: RGB black point up; Green top point down; Blue top point down
    by twice the Green amount.
- **Presetpedia, "Tokyo Neon"** https://presetpedia.com/japan-lightroom-presets/
  - Weak source.
  - Basic: Exposure -0.50; Contrast +45; Highlights -60; Shadows -20; Whites +15; Blacks -50.
  - Colour: Temperature -20; Tint +5; Vibrance +40; Saturation +15.
  - Hue: Orange +10; Aqua -15; Purple +20.
  - Saturation: Orange +40; Aqua +50; Blue +35; Purple +45; Red +30.
  - Luminance: Orange +15; Aqua +20; Blue -10.
  - Clarity +30; Dehaze +15; Vignette -35.
  - Parametric curve: Highlights -15; Lights +5; Darks -20; Shadows -25.
