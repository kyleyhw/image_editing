# Food, film stock and seasonal looks: tutorials with numeric settings

Research on 2026-09-26. Every number below was verified on the page. Values that were only in
screenshots are marked.

## 1. Bright, clean food

**Replica Surfaces, Heather Templeton, "Light and airy food photography"**
https://www.replicasurfaces.com/blogs/replica-surfaces-blog/how-to-take-light-and-airy-food-photos

- Exposure +0.25; Whites about 35; Shadows about 75.
- Directions only: Highlights all the way down; contrast, temperature, tint, vibrance.

**FoodShot AI, Ali Tanis, "Food Color Grading"** https://foodshot.ai/blog/food-color-grading

- Vendor blog.
- White balance about 5500-6000 K; Tint +5 to +10.
- Curve: black point lifted 5-10 %.
- Yellow Saturation +10 to +15; Yellow Luminance -15 to -20; Green Luminance +5 to +10.
- Red Hue +5 to +10; Red Saturation +5 to +10.
- Colour grading "fresh and clean": cool shadows at 5-8 %; Balance +10 to +20.

## 2. Dark and moody food / still life

**Flourish Presets, "How to Edit Dark and Moody Photos"**
https://flourishpresets.com/blogs/flourish-presets-lightroom-presets-luts/how-to-edit-dark-and-moody-photos-moody-presets

- Basic: Exposure -0.3 to -0.7; Contrast +30 to +50; Highlights -60 to -80; Shadows +30 to +50;
  Whites -20 to -40; Blacks -20 to -30.
- Clarity +15 to +25; Vibrance -10 to -20; Saturation -15 to -25.
- Saturation: Blues -20; Greens -30; Yellows -15.
- Split tone: cool highlights and warm shadows (hues not given); Balance -15.
- Vignette -20.
- Calibration: Shadow Tint +10; Blue primary hue -10; Blue primary saturation -20.

**MySpicyKitchen, Usha Rao, "How to Edit Food Photos in Lightroom"** (dark background)
https://www.myspicykitchen.net/lightroom-how-to-edit-food-photos-in-lightroom/

- Contrast 5-15; Shadows 28; Clarity in the teens; Vibrance below 20; Saturation 0.

**FoodShot AI, "Warm comfort food" grading:** shadows amber at 8-12 %; highlights golden at 5-8 %.

## 3. Kodak Ektar 100

**Legendary Presets, "Kodak Ektar 100 Look"** https://legendarypresets.com/kodak-ektar-100-lightroom-preset-guide/

- White balance 5200-5500 K; Tint -5 to -8; Highlights -20 to -30.
- Curve: black point +10, gentle S-curve.
- Saturation: Red +18 to +25; Orange -5 to -8; Blue +10 to +12; Green +5.
- Hue: Green -5.
- Luminance: Blue -12 to -15; Aqua -8.

**Legendary Presets, High-Saturation workflow** (same author, so not independent)
https://legendarypresets.com/step-by-step-lightroom-workflow-for-high-saturation-film-looks/

- Ektar HSL, hue / saturation / luminance:

  | Colour | Hue | Saturation | Luminance |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Red | 0 | +18 to +25 | -8 to -10 |
  | Orange | 0 | -5 to -8 | -5 |
  | Yellow | -5 | +5 | -5 |
  | Green | -5 | +5 | 0 |
  | Aqua | 0 | +8 | -8 |
  | Blue | 0 | +10 to +12 | -12 to -15 |

- Vibrance +10 to +20; Highlights -15 to -30; Whites -5 to -15.

## 4. Autumn

**WeGraphics, Diego Sanchez, "Enhance Autumn Colors in Lightroom"**
https://we.graphics/blog/enhance-autumn-colors-in-lightroom-with-ease/

The only complete recipe; the values were read from the page's screenshots.

- Basic: Temperature +5; Exposure +0.30; Contrast -20; Highlights +20; Shadows +40; Whites -20;
  Blacks -20; Clarity +10; Vibrance +30; Saturation -20.
- Curve: 0-0, 65-45, 130-120, 195-190, 255-255.
- Hue: Orange -10; Yellow -65; Green -100.
- Saturation: Red -20; Orange +30; Yellow -20; Green -40; Aqua -20; Blue -20.
- Luminance: Red +10; Orange -10; Yellow -20; Green -20; Aqua -20; Blue -20.
- Colour grading: Shadows hue 30, saturation 20; Blending 100.

**Life Pixel, "Making Fall Colors Pop"** https://www.lifepixel.com/photo-tutorials/making-fall-colors-pop-adobe-lightroom

- Vibrance +50; Saturation +40.

## 5. Winter / snow: no complete recipe found

- Mikko Lagerstedt, "Cinematic Color Grading"
  https://mikkolagerstedt.com/blog/cinematic-color-grading-landscape-photography/
  - Shadows hue 210-220, saturation 10-15.
  - Highlights hue 35-45, saturation 5-10.
  - Blue hue -10 to -15 (for snow).
- DPS, David Shaw, "Tips for Processing Winter Landscapes"
  https://digital-photography-school.com/tips-for-processing-winter-landscapes-in-lightroom/
  - Contrast +16; Clarity +62; Vignette -20 (one example image).
- Picfair Focus, James Abbott, "How to shoot and edit snowy winter landscapes"
  https://focus.picfair.com/articles/how-to-shoot-and-edit-snowy-landscapes
  - Exposure -0.10; Whites -10 to -20.
